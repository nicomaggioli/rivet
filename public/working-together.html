Working with Rivet

Built around the way you work.

We help organize your firm’s records, configure the opportunities you want to find, and work through your first proposal together.

Plan your walkthrough ↗
01

Bring your firm’s experience together.

Start with the material your team already uses. Rivet connects firm information, project history, personnel, and partners so each pursuit can draw from a shared source.

  • Firm profile, disciplines, and qualifications
  • Resumes, project sheets, and past proposal material
  • Personnel and partner records
02

Set your opportunity criteria.

Configure SAM.gov monitoring around the work your firm wants to pursue. Your criteria help shape the pipeline and the scoring used to evaluate each opportunity.

  • Relevant NAICS codes and notice types
  • Geography, set-asides, and firm priorities
  • Fit criteria your team can review and refine
03

Work through a real pursuit.

Review the analysis, select relevant projects, confirm your team, and prepare the proposal sections. An initial pursuit shows where your records are strong and where more information is needed.

  • AI analysis grounded in the solicitation
  • Project and personnel recommendations
  • SF330 preparation with your team’s review
04

Improve the next proposal.

Keep your source material current and save feedback on the language your firm prefers. Those preferences can guide future drafts, while decisions to pursue or decline help refine opportunity scoring.

  • Saved tone, terminology, and wording preferences
  • Updated project and personnel experience
  • Firm judgment that informs the next pursuit
